How to read these

Click a finding to jump to its frame and see the measurement drawn on the lifter. The checks use one side-on camera and a general body model, so joint angles can be off by several degrees, more when a plate hides a joint. Treat a flag as a reason to look at that frame, not as a verdict.

Dataset

Your labelled lifts as one file for analysis or training a model later: bar position, speed and joint angles frame by frame, with the phases, the app's findings and your labels (format: docs/dataset-format.md).

Coach

A language model running on this computer (LM Studio or Ollama) reads the analysis and suggests what to work on.

Nothing leaves this computer. The coaching notes it uses (knowledge/coaching.md) are a draft that still needs review by a qualified coach, and the model can be wrong: treat it as a second opinion.
